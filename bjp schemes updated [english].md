# TNBJP NALAM THITTAM — MASTER DIRECTORY (32 SCHEMES)

---

## TIER 1: INSTANT SPOT DELIVERY (0–15 MINUTES)

### 1
* **Short Name:** e-Shram
* **Full Title:** e-Shram Card (Unorganized Worker Registration)
* **Cluster:** Worker Welfare & Foundation
* **Benefit:** Instant Universal Account Number (UAN) card with Rs. 2 Lakh free accidental insurance.
* **Overview:** Creates a national database of unorganized workers (drivers, street vendors, laborers, domestic help) to deliver direct social security benefits and disaster relief directly via Aadhaar.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Aadhaar-linked Mobile Number, Bank Account Number & IFSC Code.

---

### 2
* **Short Name:** ABHA
* **Full Title:** Ayushman Bharat Health Account
* **Cluster:** Health
* **Benefit:** Free 14-digit digital health ID card to access and store medical records electronically.
* **Overview:** Serves as a digital gateway across all empaneled hospitals and clinics in India, allowing citizens to share medical histories and lab reports digitally without paper files.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card & Aadhaar-linked Mobile Number (or Driving License).

---

### 3
* **Short Name:** Udyam
* **Full Title:** Udyam MSME Registration
* **Cluster:** Credit & Enterprise
* **Benefit:** Free digital MSME registration certificate with QR code for priority bank loans and subsidies.
* **Overview:** Formally registers small shopkeepers, micro-businesses, and service providers with the Central MSME Ministry, unlocking government tender access and lower credit interest rates.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card (Mandatory). *PAN and GSTIN are optional for micro-businesses.*

---

### 4
* **Short Name:** PMSBY
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana
* **Cluster:** Insurance
* **Benefit:** Rs. 2 Lakh accidental death / disability cover for Rs. 20/year.
* **Overview:** A low-cost annual accidental insurance scheme for savings bank account holders aged 18–70 years with automatic bank auto-debit consent.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Savings Bank Account details, Bank-registered Mobile Number.

---

### 5
* **Short Name:** PMJJBY
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Jeevan Jyoti Bima Yojana
* **Cluster:** Insurance
* **Benefit:** Rs. 2 Lakh life insurance cover for Rs. 436/year.
* **Overview:** Provides renewable one-year term life insurance coverage for citizens aged 18–50 years, payable to the nominee upon the insured's death due to any cause.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Savings Bank Account details, Bank-registered Mobile Number.

---

### 6
* **Short Name:** Jan Dhan
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)
* **Cluster:** Financial Inclusion & Foundation
* **Benefit:** Zero-balance bank account with RuPay debit card and built-in Rs. 2 Lakh accident cover.
* **Overview:** The primary digital foundation layer for all central government Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) cash subsidies, eliminating intermediaries and bank minimum balance penalties.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card (or Voter ID / NREGA Card) + Passport Photo.

---

### 7
* **Short Name:** PM-POSHAN Tracker
* **Full Title:** PM-POSHAN Student Entitlement Tracker
* **Cluster:** Education & Child Care
* **Benefit:** Instant verification of free school meals, uniforms, textbooks, and footwears.
* **Overview:** Enables parents of students in Classes 1–8 in Tamil Nadu government and aided schools to instantly track and confirm their children's nutritional and material welfare entitlements.
* **Documents:** Student EMIS Number or Family Smart Ration Card / Aadhaar Number.

---

## TIER 2: FAST-TRACK CLEARANCE (1–3 DAYS)

### 8
* **Short Name:** Ayushman Bharat / CMCHIS
* **Full Title:** Ayushman Bharat PM Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY)
* **Cluster:** Health
* **Benefit:** Up to Rs. 5 Lakh/year cashless hospitalization cover per family.
* **Overview:** Integrated with Tamil Nadu's Chief Minister's Comprehensive Health Insurance Scheme (CMCHIS) to provide financial protection against secondary and tertiary medical emergencies.
* **Documents:** Tamil Nadu Smart Ration Card (Family Card), Aadhaar Cards of all family members, Active Mobile Number.

---

### 9
* **Short Name:** Ayushman 70+
* **Full Title:** Ayushman Bharat Senior Citizen Top-Up Scheme
* **Cluster:** Senior Citizen Health
* **Benefit:** Exclusive Rs. 5 Lakh/year cashless health cover for senior citizens aged 70+.
* **Overview:** Provides an independent health cover to all elderly citizens aged 70 and above, regardless of income level, without consuming their family's standard Ayushman card limit.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card (showing age 70+), Mobile Number linked to Aadhaar.

---

### 10
* **Short Name:** PM Kisan
* **Full Title:** PM Kisan Samman Nidhi
* **Cluster:** Farmers
* **Benefit:** Direct cash transfer of Rs. 6,000/year in 3 equal installments.
* **Overview:** Provides direct income support to landholding farmer families across Tamil Nadu directly into their Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Land Record details (Patta/Chitta Number), Aadhaar-seeded Bank Account.

---

### 11
* **Short Name:** PM Matru Vandana
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)
* **Cluster:** Women & Families
* **Benefit:** Rs. 5,000 direct cash benefit for pregnant and lactating mothers.
* **Overview:** Co-branded in Tamil Nadu with the Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy Maternity Scheme to compensate for wage loss and support maternal health and nutrition during pregnancy.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Bank Account details, Mother and Child Protection (MCP) Card / PICME (RCH) ID issued by Village Health Nurse (VHN).

---

### 12
* **Short Name:** APY
* **Full Title:** Atal Pension Yojana
* **Cluster:** Insurance & Pension
* **Benefit:** Guaranteed monthly pension of Rs. 1,000–Rs. 5,000 after age 60.
* **Overview:** A government-backed pension scheme for unorganized sector workers aged 18–40 years that collects small monthly contributions via bank auto-debit.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Savings Bank Account number, Bank-registered Mobile Number.

---

### 13
* **Short Name:** NAPS
* **Full Title:** National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme
* **Cluster:** Youth & Employment
* **Benefit:** Government stipend reimbursement up to Rs. 1,500/month during industrial training.
* **Overview:** Connects ITI, Diploma, and Degree graduates with industrial units across Tamil Nadu, subsidizing their practical training stipends.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Educational Certificates, Bank Account details.

---

## TIER 3: MEDIUM-RUN CLEARANCE (1–2 WEEKS)

### 14
* **Short Name:** PMKVY
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana
* **Cluster:** Youth & Skills
* **Benefit:** Free skill training, government certification, and assessment across 300+ trades.
* **Overview:** Provides industry-relevant skill training to unemployed youth and school dropouts to boost employability and placement opportunities.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Educational Marksheets, Bank Account details, Passport Photo.

---

### 15
* **Short Name:** PM Kisan Maan Dhan
* **Full Title:** PM Kisan Maan Dhan Yojana
* **Cluster:** Farmers
* **Benefit:** Guaranteed farmer pension of Rs. 3,000/month after age 60.
* **Overview:** A voluntary contributory pension scheme for small and marginal farmers owning up to 2 hectares of cultivable land in Tamil Nadu.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Patta/Chitta Land Record copy, Savings Bank Account / Jan Dhan details.

---

### 16
* **Short Name:** NSP Scholarship
* **Full Title:** National Scholarship Portal (Pre & Post Matric)
* **Cluster:** Youth & Education
* **Benefit:** Direct bank cash scholarships from Class 1 to PhD level.
* **Overview:** A single digital window for distributing various central pre-matric and post-matric government scholarships to eligible SC, ST, OBC, and minority students.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate, Community Certificate, Previous Year Marksheet, Fee Receipt, Bank Passbook.

---

### 17
* **Short Name:** PM-YASASVI
* **Full Title:** PM Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme
* **Cluster:** Youth & Education
* **Benefit:** Scholarship of Rs. 75,000 to Rs. 1,25,000/year for school education.
* **Overview:** Financial support for meritorious OBC, EBC, and DNT students studying in Classes 9 through 12 in Tamil Nadu schools whose family income is below Rs. 2.5 Lakh/year.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Income Certificate, OBC/EBC Community Certificate, Marksheet.

---

### 18
* **Short Name:** NMMS
* **Full Title:** National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship Scheme
* **Cluster:** Youth & Education
* **Benefit:** Rs. 12,000/year scholarship (Rs. 1,000/month) from Class 9 to 12.
* **Overview:** Prevents dropouts by supporting economically weaker students in government and government-aided schools who pass the NMMS exam.
* **Documents:** Class 8 Marksheet, NMMS Selection Roll Number, Income Certificate, Aadhaar, Bank Account.

---

### 19
* **Short Name:** KCC
* **Full Title:** Kisan Credit Card (Agri / Animal Husbandry / Fisheries)
* **Cluster:** Farmers & Credit
* **Benefit:** Subsidized short-term revolving credit up to Rs. 3 Lakh at 4% effective interest.
* **Overview:** Provides working capital credit for seeds, fertilizers, livestock feed, and fishing equipment with flexible repayment schedules.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Land Ownership documents (Patta/Chitta) or Dairy/Fisheries license proof, Bank Account details.

---

### 20
* **Short Name:** DAY-NRLM
* **Full Title:** Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana - National Rural Livelihoods Mission
* **Cluster:** Women Empowerment
* **Benefit:** Revolving Fund (Rs. 15,000) and Community Investment Fund (Rs. 1.5 Lakh) for Self-Help Groups.
* **Overview:** Mobilizes rural women into Self-Help Groups (SHGs) under Mahalir Thittam, providing financial support and bank credit linkage for local micro-enterprises.
* **Documents:** SHG Registration Copy, Member Aadhaar Card, Smart Ration Card, SHG Bank Account details.

---

### 21
* **Short Name:** Sukanya Samriddhi
* **Full Title:** Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (Selvamagal Semmippu)
* **Cluster:** Women & Families
* **Benefit:** High-interest savings account for girl child education and marriage.
* **Overview:** A government-backed savings scheme for girls under 10 years old, accessible via Post Offices and nationalized banks across Tamil Nadu.
* **Documents:** Girl Child Birth Certificate, Parent/Guardian Aadhaar & PAN Card, Passport Photos.

---

### 22
* **Short Name:** PM Ujjwala
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)
* **Cluster:** Women & Energy
* **Benefit:** Free LPG gas connection (stove + cylinder) plus ongoing cylinder subsidies.
* **Overview:** Delivers clean cooking fuel connections to adult women from BPL households, replacing unhealthy traditional biomass fuels.
* **Documents:** Smart Ration Card, Aadhaar Cards of all adult family members, Bank Account details, Existing Address Proof.

---

## TIER 4: MULTI-STEP VERIFICATION (3–4 WEEKS / 30+ DAYS)

### 23
* **Short Name:** PM SVANidhi
* **Full Title:** PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi
* **Cluster:** Credit & Enterprise
* **Benefit:** Collateral-free working capital loan of Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 50,000.
* **Overview:** Empowers urban street vendors and hawkers to restart or expand their businesses with low-interest, interest-subsidized bank credit.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Smart Ration Card, ULB Vending Certificate / Letter of Recommendation (LoR), Bank Account details.

---

### 24
* **Short Name:** PM Surya Ghar
* **Full Title:** PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana
* **Cluster:** Energy & Housing
* **Benefit:** Direct subsidy up to Rs. 78,000 + up to 300 units free electricity/month.
* **Overview:** Subsidizes residential rooftop solar installations, allowing households to generate green energy and sell excess electricity back to TANGEDCO.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Latest Electricity Bill (TANGEDCO), Bank Passbook, Roof Photograph.

---

### 25
* **Short Name:** PM-KUSUM
* **Full Title:** PM Kisan Urja Suraksha evam Utthaan Mahabhiyan
* **Cluster:** Farmers & Energy
* **Benefit:** 60% subsidy for installing off-grid solar agricultural water pumps.
* **Overview:** Reduces diesel dependencies and power costs for Tamil Nadu farmers by solarizing agricultural irrigation pumps.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Patta/Chitta Land Records, TANGEDCO Agri Power Connection details (if applicable), Bank Passbook.

---

### 26
* **Short Name:** PMMSY
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana
* **Cluster:** Fisheries & Coastal
* **Benefit:** 40% to 60% capital subsidy for boats, safety gear, and aquaculture units.
* **Overview:** Modernizes Tamil Nadu's marine and inland fisheries sector, strengthening cold chain logistics, safety equipment, and fish farming infrastructure.
* **Documents:** Fisherman ID / Biometric Card, Aadhaar, Bank Account, Land/Waterbody Lease document (for aquaculture).

---

### 27
* **Short Name:** PM-FME
* **Full Title:** PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises
* **Cluster:** Credit & Enterprise
* **Benefit:** 35% credit-linked capital subsidy up to Rs. 10 Lakhs.
* **Overview:** Assists micro food processing units (pickle, spice, snack, rice processing) with business upgrades, food safety compliance, and packaging.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Business Address Proof, FSSAI License (or application), Detailed Project Report (DPR), Bank Account.

---

### 28
* **Short Name:** PM Mudra
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (Shishu & Kishor)
* **Cluster:** Credit & Enterprise
* **Benefit:** Collateral-free business loan up to Rs. 50,000 (Shishu) or Rs. 5 Lakh (Kishor).
* **Overview:** Provides non-corporate, non-farm micro and small enterprises with commercial bank credit for inventory and capital expansion.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Business Proof (Udyam Cert / Rent agreement / Trade license), Bank Account statement (last 6 months).

---

### 29
* **Short Name:** PM Awas Yojana
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY-G / PMAY-U)
* **Cluster:** Housing
* **Benefit:** Financial grant of Rs. 1.2 Lakh to Rs. 2.67 Lakh for building or upgrading a house.
* **Overview:** Assists rural and urban homeless families and kutcha house residents to construct durable, permanent pucca homes equipped with basic amenities.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Cards of all family members, Smart Ration Card, Income Certificate, Land Ownership / Patta Copy, Bank Account details.

---

### 30
* **Short Name:** Stand Up India
* **Full Title:** Stand Up India Scheme
* **Cluster:** Credit & Enterprise
* **Benefit:** Bank loan from Rs. 10 Lakh to Rs. 1 Crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs.
* **Overview:** Facilitates bank loans for setting up greenfield manufacturing, services, or trading enterprises by marginalized groups and women.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar, PAN Card, Category Certificate (if SC/ST), Company Registration, Detailed Project Report (DPR), Bank Statements.

---

### 31
* **Short Name:** PM Fasal Bima
* **Full Title:** Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
* **Cluster:** Farmers
* **Benefit:** Comprehensive crop insurance against natural calamities, pests, and diseases.
* **Overview:** Protects Tamil Nadu farmers against yield losses with nominal premium rates (1.5%–2%), ensuring crop income stability.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Land Patta/Chitta / Sowing Certificate from Village Administrative Officer (VAO), Bank Account details.

---

### 32
* **Short Name:** PMEGP
* **Full Title:** Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme
* **Cluster:** Credit & Enterprise
* **Benefit:** Credit-linked subsidy up to 35% for new micro-enterprise setup (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs project cost).
* **Overview:** Promotes self-employment through non-farm micro-enterprises in manufacturing and services, administered via KVIC and commercial banks.
* **Documents:** Aadhaar Card, Educational Certificate, EDP Training Cert (if completed), Detailed Project Report (DPR), Community Certificate.
