## Credit & Enterprise

### Udyam
*Udyam MSME Registration*
Free digital MSME registration certificate with QR code for priority bank loans and subsidies.
**Required docs:** Aadhaar Card (Mandatory). PAN and GSTIN are optional for micro-businesses

### PM SVANidhi
*PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi*
Collateral-free working capital loan of Rs. 10,000 to Rs. 50,000.
**Required docs:** Aadhaar Card Smart Ration Card ULB Vending Certificate / Letter of Recommendation (LoR) Bank Account details

### PM-FME
*PM Formalisation of Micro Food Processing Enterprises*
35% credit-linked capital subsidy up to Rs. 10 Lakhs.
**Required docs:** Aadhaar Card PAN Card Business Address Proof FSSAI License (or application) Detailed Project Report (DPR) Bank Account

### PM Mudra
*Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (Shishu & Kishor)*
Collateral-free business loan up to Rs. 50,000 (Shishu) or Rs. 5 Lakh (Kishor).
**Required docs:** Aadhaar Card Voter ID Business Proof (Udyam Cert / Rent agreement / Trade license) Bank Account statement (last 6 months)

### Stand Up India
*Stand Up India Scheme*
Bank loan from Rs. 10 Lakh to Rs. 1 Crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs.
**Required docs:** Aadhaar PAN Card Category Certificate (if SC/ST) Company Registration Detailed Project Report (DPR) Bank Statements

### PMEGP
*Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme*
Credit-linked subsidy up to 35% for new micro-enterprise setup (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs project cost).
**Required docs:** Aadhaar Card Educational Certificate EDP Training Cert (if completed) Detailed Project Report (DPR) Community Certificate
